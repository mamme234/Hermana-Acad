🎯 Our Purpose & Mission

Why I Created This Website

As a passionate advocate for educational technology in Ethiopia, I recognized a critical gap in how schools manage their administrative processes. Many schools still rely on paper-based systems, manual fee collection, and traditional communication methods that are inefficient and time-consuming.


🎯 My Vision: To create a comprehensive digital ecosystem that bridges the gap between students, parents, teachers, and school administration.


💡 The Problem I'm Solving:

  • 📝 Manual Registration: Long queues and paperwork for student enrollment
  • 💰 Payment Challenges: No digital record of fee payments and receipts
  • 🪪 Identity Issues: Students lack proper digital ID cards
  • 📊 Data Management: Directors can't track student progress in real-time
  • 👪 Parent Communication: No centralized system for school updates

🚀 My Solution - Hermana Academy Platform:

  • ✅ Digital Registration: Students can register online with photo upload
  • ✅ Smart Exam System: Grade-appropriate ability testing with automatic scoring
  • ✅ Secure Payments: Digital fee collection with instant receipts
  • ✅ Professional ID Cards: Ethiopian-style identification cards for every student
  • ✅ Director Dashboard: Complete oversight of all students and payments
  • ✅ Parent Hub: Real-time updates about school activities

🌟 Future Goals:

  • 📱 Mobile App for easier access
  • 💳 Integration with Ethiopian banking systems (Telebirr, CBE Birr)
  • 📚 Online learning modules and video lessons
  • 🤖 AI-powered student performance analytics
  • 💬 Parent-Teacher messaging system

"Empowering Ethiopian education through technology - one student at a time."

— Muhammad Ilyas, Founder & Developer

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024


At Hermana Academy, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our educational management platform.


📌 Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Full name, Ethiopian ID number, email address, phone number
  • Academic Information: Grade level, exam scores, academic progress
  • Payment Information: Fee payment history, transaction IDs, payment dates
  • Photographic Data: Student photo for ID card generation
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our platform

🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • ✅ To process student registrations and create ID cards
  • ✅ To administer and grade ability examinations
  • ✅ To process fee payments and generate official receipts
  • ✅ To communicate important school announcements and updates
  • ✅ To provide directors with analytics on student performance
  • ✅ To improve our services and user experience

🛡️ Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. All personal data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage, and no data is shared with third parties without your explicit consent.


📱 Your Rights

  • ✔️ Right to access your personal data
  • ✔️ Right to correct inaccurate data
  • ✔️ Right to delete your account and associated data
  • ✔️ Right to data portability

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to remember your login session, preferences, and to analyze site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.
