📖 About Hermana Academy

Hermana Academy is a premier educational institution in Ethiopia, dedicated to providing quality education and fostering academic excellence.


🏫 Our History

Founded in 2015, Hermana Academy has grown from a small community school to one of Ethiopia's most respected educational institutions, serving over 2,000 students across all grade levels.


🎯 Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that prepare them for success in higher education and life, while preserving Ethiopian cultural heritage.


🌟 Our Vision

To become Ethiopia's leading educational institution, recognized for academic excellence, innovation, and character development.


🏆 Achievements

  • Top 3 in National Exam Results (2022, 2023, 2024)
  • Best Digital School Award 2024
  • 95% University Admission Rate
  • Ethiopian Green School Certification

👨‍🏫 Our Team

We have 150+ qualified teachers, 30 administrative staff, and a dedicated leadership team committed to student success.

🎯 Our Purpose - Why I Created This Website

Hello! I created this website with a clear vision and purpose:

Digital Transformation

To bring Ethiopian education into the digital age by providing an online platform for student management, registration, and payments.

Accessibility

To make school administration accessible to all parents and students, reducing paperwork and saving time.

Transparency

To create transparency in fee payments, student progress tracking, and school communications.

Empower Students

To give students a digital identity with ID cards, exam tracking, and easy access to their academic records.

Parent Engagement

To keep parents informed about school updates, student conduct, and important announcements in real-time.

Data-Driven Decisions

To provide directors with real-time data on student enrollment, payment status, and academic performance.

💡 My Inspiration

As someone who believes in the power of education, I saw the need for a modern, efficient school management system in Ethiopia. Many schools still rely on paper records, manual fee collection, and parent-teacher meetings as the only communication channel. This website bridges that gap by bringing everything online - registration, payments, ID cards, and updates - all in one place.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024


📌 Information We Collect

We collect personal information including: full name, Ethiopian ID, email, grade level, payment records, and student photo for ID cards.


🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • Student registration and identification
  • Processing fee payments and generating receipts

🛡️ Data Protection
