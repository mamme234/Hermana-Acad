🎯 Our Purpose & Vision

Hermana Academy was founded with a clear mission: to revolutionize education in Ethiopia by digitizing school management, making quality learning accessible from Nursery to Grade 12.


🎓 Our Core Purpose: To provide a secure, transparent, and efficient platform where students, parents, teachers, and administrators can seamlessly interact. We believe every child deserves a bright future, and technology is the bridge.


🇪🇹 Vision: To become Ethiopia's leading digital education hub, producing future leaders equipped with 21st-century skills while preserving our rich cultural heritage.


📚 Key Objectives:

  • ✅ Streamline student registration and ID management
  • ✅ Enable secure online exams with proctoring
  • ✅ Facilitate transparent fee payments and receipts
  • ✅ Empower teachers with digital tools
  • ✅ Provide real-time parent portals

🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

At Hermana Academy, your privacy is our priority. We collect only essential data for academic and administrative purposes.


📌 What we collect: Name, Ethiopian ID, contact info, academic records, payment history, and exam proctoring images (for security).

🔐 How we protect: All data is encrypted locally and never shared with third parties. Exam photos are used only for verification.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Rights: Parents can access their child's records anytime. You may request data deletion by contacting us.

📜 Compliance: We adhere to Ethiopian education data protection standards. Data is stored securely in your browser's local storage.


⏰ Data Retention: Student records are kept until graduation + 5 years. Teacher records are kept for 7 years.
