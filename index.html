💡 The Problems I Solved - My Journey as a Developer

👋 Hello, I'm Muhammad Ilyas. Let me tell you why I built Hermana Academy.


🔴 The Problem That Started It All:

In 2024, I visited a small school in Addis Ababa. I saw teachers manually writing student records in dusty notebooks. ID cards were handwritten on paper that got lost within weeks. Exam cheating was rampant because there was no proctoring system. Parents had no way to check their child's progress. The director couldn't track payments. Teachers applied with paper forms that got lost in drawers. I knew I had to build something better.


⚙️ Problem #1 - No Centralized Student Management

Schools had student data scattered across multiple notebooks and Excel files. Finding a student's history took hours. ✅ I SOLVED THIS by creating a complete digital profile for every student with Ethiopian ID, photo upload, grade tracking, exam scores, and payment history - all in one place.


🔒 Problem #2 - Exam Cheating & No Security

Students would cheat during entrance exams. Teachers couldn't verify who was actually taking the test. ✅ I SOLVED THIS by building an ultra-secure exam system that: takes your photo before starting, disables right-click and keyboard shortcuts, prevents tab switching, detects window blur, records violations, and terminates the exam after 3 violations.


🪪 Problem #3 - Fake / Lost ID Cards

Schools spent thousands of birr printing plastic ID cards that got lost or damaged. ✅ I SOLVED THIS by creating digital ID cards with Ethiopian flag design, downloadable as PNG, and printable - all generated instantly after registration and exam passage.


💰 Problem #4 - Payment Confusion & Lost Receipts

Parents paid fees but received paper receipts that faded or got lost. Schools had no backup. ✅ I SOLVED THIS with a clear payment dashboard showing registration (1000 ETB), term+bus packages (3500 ETB each), downloadable digital receipts, and automatic email confirmations.


👨‍🏫 Problem #5 - Teacher Application Chaos

Paper applications got lost in piles. No tracking system. Qualified teachers had no way to check their status. ✅ I SOLVED THIS with digital teacher registration including document upload, exam proctoring, teaching grades selection, and a board approval workflow with status tracking (pending/approved/rejected) and automatic email notifications.


👪 Problem #6 - Parents Had No Visibility

Parents had to physically visit the school to see their child's grades or payment status. ✅ I SOLVED THIS by creating a parent portal where they can log in with their child's Student ID and view everything - exam scores, payment history, teacher assignments, and school announcements.


📊 Problem #7 - Directors Had No Overview

School directors had no dashboard showing student distribution by grade, teacher performance, or financial overview. ✅ I SOLVED THIS with separate director dashboards for KG, Elementary, and High School levels - showing students under their command, teachers, and revenue statistics.


📧 Problem #8 - No Digital Communication

Schools had no way to send official communications to parents and teachers. ✅ I SOLVED THIS by integrating real email notifications for registration confirmations, exam results, Student IDs, approval codes, payment receipts, and application status updates.


🌟 What I Learned: Building this system taught me that technology can genuinely transform education. Every feature I added came from watching real people struggle with real problems. This is not just code - it's a solution to real Ethiopian educational challenges.


My Mission Continues: I will keep improving Hermana Academy. It's free, it's secure, and it's built for Ethiopia.

🔒 Privacy Policy - Your Data is Safe With Me

Last Updated: June 2026


📌 What Data Does Hermana Academy Collect?

  • Students: Full Name, Ethiopian ID, email address, phone number, grade level, parent/guardian information, home address, photograph, exam scores, violation records, payment history.
  • Teachers: Full Name, email address, phone number, education documents, teaching grades, subject specialization, years of experience, photograph, exam scores, application status.
  • Parents: Only viewing access to their child's data - no separate data collection.

🔐 How Is Your Data Protected?

  • ✅ All passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing - never stored in plain text.
  • ✅ Exam photos are captured live and only used for identity verification during the exam.
  • ✅ All data is stored in secure MongoDB databases with access controls.
  • ✅ No third-party access - ever. Your data never leaves our secure servers.
  • ✅ HTTPS encryption protects all data transmission.

📸 Exam Proctoring Privacy:

When you take an exam, your photo is captured via your camera. This photo is used ONLY to verify your identity during the exam. It is not shared, sold, or used for any other purpose. You can request deletion at any time.


👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Rights:

Parents have full viewing access to their child's academic records, exam results, and payment status. Parents cannot modify any data. If you believe any information is incorrect, please contact the school administration.


🗑️ Data Deletion Request:

You can request complete deletion of your data by contacting me directly. I will remove all your information within 7 business days. Email: privacy@hermanaacademy.edu.et
