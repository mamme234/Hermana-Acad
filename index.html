🎯 Our Purpose & Vision

Why We Created Hermana Academy

As passionate educators and technologists, we created Hermana Academy to revolutionize education in Ethiopia. Our journey began with a simple observation: traditional school management was drowning in paperwork, inefficiency, and lack of transparency.


🎯 Our Core Mission

To provide every Ethiopian student with access to quality education through innovative technology, transparent administration, and dedicated educators.


💡 What Drives Us

  • Digital Transformation: Moving from paper-based systems to seamless digital experience accessible from anywhere
  • Financial Transparency: Enabling parents to track payments, download receipts, and monitor fee status in real-time
  • Student Empowerment: Providing digital ID cards, exam tracking, and academic records at students' fingertips
  • Teacher Support: Streamlining application process, document management, and communication with administration
  • Parent Engagement: Keeping families informed about student progress, school updates, and important announcements
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Giving directors and board members real-time analytics for better school management

🌟 Our Vision for the Future: To become Ethiopia's leading digital-first educational institution, setting the standard for school management and student success across the nation.


🚀 2025 Goals: Expand to 5,000+ students, integrate with Ethiopian banks for real payments, launch mobile app, and implement AI-powered learning analytics.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 | Last Updated: June 2024


At Hermana Academy, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.


📌 Information We Collect

We collect personal information including:

  • Full name, Ethiopian ID, email address, phone number
  • Grade level, academic records, exam scores and violations
  • Student photographs for ID cards and verification
  • Payment history, transaction records, and receipts
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Teacher credentials, education documents, and employment history

🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • Student registration and identification verification
  • Processing fee payments and generating official receipts
  • Creating secure student ID cards with Ethiopian branding
  • Communicating school updates and emergency announcements
  • Academic tracking, exam proctoring, and performance monitoring
  • Teacher application review and credential verification
  • Improving our services and user experience

🛡️ Data Protection & Security

All data is encrypted using AES-256 industry-standard protocols. Access to personal information is strictly limited to authorized school personnel. We use secure servers hosted in Ethiopia with regular security audits and updates.


📱 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information. You may request a copy of your data or withdraw consent at any time. Contact our Data Protection Officer for privacy-related requests.


🍪 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to remember your login session and preferences. You can disable cookies in browser settings, but this may affect functionality.
