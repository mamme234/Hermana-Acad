🎯 My Purpose & Vision

My name is Muhammad Ilyas, and I created Hermana Academy with a clear vision for Ethiopia's educational future.

As a passionate software developer and education advocate, I witnessed firsthand the challenges faced by Ethiopian schools: endless paperwork, manual fee collection, lost receipts, communication gaps between parents and teachers, and no centralized system for student records. Traditional methods were inefficient, time-consuming, and prone to errors.


💡 The Problem I Saw

  • 📝 Paper-based systems - Student registrations lost, records damaged, information inaccessible
  • 💰 Payment confusion - Parents unsure about fee status, no digital receipts, payment disputes
  • 🎓 No student identity - No standardized ID cards, difficulty verifying student enrollment
  • 📊 Limited oversight - Directors and boards lacked real-time data on school operations
  • 👨‍🏫 Teacher application delays - Manual document processing, no application tracking
  • 👪 Parent disconnect - Parents uninformed about school updates and student progress

🚀 The Solution - Hermana Academy

I built this complete School Management System to solve these problems with:

  • ✅ Digital Registration - Students register online with photo upload and Ethiopian ID verification
  • ✅ Secure Timed Exams - 1-minute proctored exams with auto-submit and violation tracking
  • ✅ Instant Student ID Cards - Ethiopian-style digital ID cards generated upon passing exams
  • ✅ Receipt-Based Payments - Students upload payment receipts, finance team verifies them
  • ✅ Teacher Application Portal - Teachers apply online, boards review and approve digitally
  • ✅ Real-Time Dashboards - Directors and boards see live statistics on students, payments, and teachers
  • ✅ Parent Communication Hub - Parents access child's progress and school announcements instantly

🌟 My Vision for Ethiopia: I dream of a digitally transformed Ethiopian education system where every school has efficient management, every student has a digital identity, every payment is transparent, and every parent is informed. Hermana Academy is my contribution to this digital future.

🚀 Future Roadmap: Mobile app launch • Integration with Ethiopian banks (CBE, Dashen, Awash) • AI-powered student performance analytics • Online learning modules • Parent-teacher video conferencing • Multi-language support (Amharic, English, Afaan Oromo)

This system is my humble gift to Ethiopian education. I believe technology can bridge gaps, create opportunities, and build a brighter future for every Ethiopian child. 🇪🇹 ኢትዮጵያ ትበልጥ! (Ethiopia First!)

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 | Last Updated: June 2024


At Hermana Academy, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our School Management System. Please read this policy carefully.


📌 Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Full name, Ethiopian ID number, date of birth
  • Email address, phone number, residential address
  • Student photographs for ID cards and verification
  • Parent/guardian names and contact information
  • Payment history, transaction records, and receipts

Academic Information:

  • Grade level, class assignments, enrollment dates
  • Exam scores, completion status, violation records
  • Student ID numbers and academic progress

Teacher Information:

  • Education documents, teaching certificates
  • Teaching grades, experience, application details
  • Salary records and employment history

🔐 How We Use Your Information

  • Student registration and identity verification
  • Processing fee payments and generating official receipts
  • Creating and managing student ID cards
  • Administering secure timed exams and recording results
  • Communicating school updates, announcements, and emergency alerts
  • Reviewing teacher applications and credentials
  • Generating reports for directors and boards
  • Improving our services and user experience

🛡️ Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted using AES-256 standards
  • Secure Servers: Hosted on protected servers with firewalls
  • Access Control: Role-based access - only authorized personnel can view specific data
  • Regular Audits: Weekly security audits and vulnerability scans
  • Backup: Daily encrypted backups to prevent data loss

📱 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of communications at any time

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to remember your login session, preferences, and improve performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.


👶 Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at educational institutions. For student users, we collect information only with parental consent or through the school administration. Parents have full access to their child's information.
